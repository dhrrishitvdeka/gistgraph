# Code of Conduct

This project is for doing careful research in the open. To keep it a place where that is possible:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas and evidence, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear before assuming the worst.
- Keep discussion on the technical merits. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and
  unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.
- Give credit, cite prior work, and do not misrepresent results.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, email
dhrrishitvdeka@duck.com or open a private report through the repository's Security tab. Reports are
handled in confidence.

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any official
channel.
